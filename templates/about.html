The story behind the blog

Hi, I'm Ibunu Suhudhu — a passionate developer and writer who enjoys building web applications and exploring creative ideas through technology and storytelling.

This blog website was created as a Flask-based project where users can create, edit, and manage blog posts with a clean and simple interface. The project focuses on backend development concepts such as routing, databases, form handling, CRUD operations, and dynamic content rendering.

Alongside programming, I also enjoy writing emotional and story-driven content, which inspired the design and feel of this blog platform.

Thank you for visiting and exploring my project.
